# 14. Use Cases

The deliberative process is the same for every session. What changes is the question — and the decision that rides on the answer.

## 14.1 Prediction Markets

The launch context. Prediction markets — platforms like Polymarket and Kalshi — host binary and probabilistic questions where real money is wagered on resolution. The quality of analysis directly determines outcomes over time, and the resolution mechanism gives every verdict a ground-truth check.

Where Augle helps:
- Evaluating contested political and economic questions with conflicting evidence
- Stress-testing a position before committing capital
- Understanding what the market is pricing correctly and where it is wrong
- Building a documented rationale for a high-conviction trade
- Comparing similar contracts across platforms (Polymarket vs Kalshi calibration)
- Identifying contracts where the resolution mechanics differ from the apparent question

## 14.2 Venture Capital and Investment Research

A VC fund evaluating a deal, an analyst building an investment thesis, or an individual investor researching a position faces the same structural problem: contested evidence, uncertain timelines, and strong incentives to find the answer you want to find.

Where Augle helps:
- Stress-testing an investment thesis against the strongest available counterarguments
- Mapping the evidence landscape for an emerging market or technology
- Identifying the assumptions a thesis depends on — and whether they are defensible
- Producing documented analysis that holds up in a partner meeting or LP update
- Pre-mortem analysis on a deal before commitment
- Comparative diligence across multiple companies in a contested space

## 14.3 Legal Research and Analysis

Legal arguments are deliberative by nature — adversarial, evidence-grounded, and structured around contested claims. Augle's process mirrors the structure of legal reasoning in a way few AI tools do.

Where Augle helps:
- Mapping precedent and counterargument on a contested legal question
- Stress-testing an argument before it reaches opposing counsel
- Identifying the weakest links in a case theory
- Synthesizing conflicting interpretations of statute or regulation
- Preparing for cross-examination by anticipating the strongest objections

## 14.4 Academic Research

Researchers working at the frontier of a field face genuine uncertainty about what the evidence shows. A deliberative process that maps contested claims, surfaces hidden assumptions, and explicitly flags knowledge gaps is exactly what rigorous research requires. Letters and Science (v2) is built specifically for this user.

Where Augle helps:
- Testing a hypothesis against the strongest available counterevidence
- Synthesizing conflicting literature on a contested empirical question
- Identifying gaps in the existing research that a new study could address
- Preparing a defensible answer to the hardest objections a paper will face
- Cross-referencing claims against retraction databases (v2 SVS)
- Building a literature review with confidence-graded evidence nodes

## 14.5 Enterprise Strategy

Strategic decisions are hard not because the right answer is hidden — it is usually visible in the evidence, if you look at it honestly. The difficulty is that the people making the decision have strong incentives to see the evidence a certain way.

Where Augle helps:
- Pre-mortems on major strategic initiatives before commitment
- Competitive landscape analysis that accounts for adversarial pressure
- Market entry analysis for new geographies or verticals
- Scenario planning with explicit uncertainty mapping
- Board-ready analysis with documented adversarial review

## 14.6 What These Use Cases Share

Every use case shares one characteristic: a consequential question that the available tools handle poorly. A single AI model. A search engine. A consultant with a deadline and an incentive to sound confident.

Augle is the alternative — structured, adversarial, traceable, and honest about what it does not know.
