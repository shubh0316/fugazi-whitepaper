# 17. Team

Augle is built by Augle, Inc., a Delaware C-corporation. We are a two-person founding team — one building the product, one building the company — and we are moving fast.

## 17.1 Cory Kelly

**Co-Founder & CEO · Product Visionary · UI/UX Product Design**

Cory leads strategy, product vision, UI/UX design, investor relations, and go-to-market. He brings 15 years of digital product design across complex domains — including healthcare systems and enterprise workflows — with hands-on experience leading teams of 20+ developers and designers from concept through delivery. His working style favors bold decisions made fast and adjusted on evidence, with a track record of translating abstract product vision into interfaces that actually ship.

The conviction behind Augle's deliberative architecture came from watching AI tools systematically fail at the one thing that matters most: reasoning carefully under uncertainty, and being honest about the limits of what they know. The full UX surface — every screen specification, every interaction pattern, the dual-mode color system, the Guardian visibility model, the Results screen output structure; everything was designed to make rigorous reasoning legible to users who do not need to know the architecture to trust it.

## 17.2 Shubhanker Saxena

**Co-Founder & CTO · Architect · Developer**

Shub handles all development. He designed and built the Augle architecture — the multi-agent orchestration layer, the credit and session management system, the Guardian routing logic, the Source Verification Service, the deterministic confidence verdict computation, and the UI implementation across both platform modes.

Based in India, Shub brings a deep technical foundation and product instincts that have been pressure-tested across the full Augle architecture design process. The decisions in this whitepaper are his to implement — and the quality of the implementation reflects the quality of the engineer.

## 17.3 Built on Conviction

We did not build Augle because the market told us to. We built it because we saw the gap clearly — between what AI tools currently offer and what rigorous reasoning actually requires — and we had the conviction that filling that gap was both technically possible and commercially significant.

The architecture in this whitepaper is the result of months of design work, adversarial pressure-testing, and deliberate tradeoff decisions. It is not a draft. It is a locked foundation that we are building on.
