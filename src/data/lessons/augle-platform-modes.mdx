# 10. Platform Modes

Augle ships in two platform modes. The deliberative engine is identical across both. What differs is the question source, the output framing, the Guardian safety criteria, and the visual treatment.

## 10.1 Prediction Markets (v1, live)

Prediction Markets is Augle's launch mode. Questions are sourced from the Polymarket and Kalshi APIs — structured contract data with contract IDs, current implied probabilities, close dates, and resolution status. Output is probability-oriented: probability YES/NO, confidence high/moderate/low, evidence anchored to economic data, regulatory filings, and platform-specific signals.

The visual mode is dark. The Guardian safety criteria emphasize financial regulatory boundaries — the Guardian and Synthesizer have explicit behavioral rules to ensure output is framed as analytical content, not investment advice. Every result page carries a legal disclaimer confirming Augle does not constitute investment advice.

## 10.2 Letters and Science (v2, waitlist)

Letters and Science is Augle's research and academic mode. Questions are free-form text input rather than API-sourced — a researcher posing a hypothesis, an academic working through contested literature, an analyst evaluating a contested empirical question. The Topic Architect runs a neutrality check on the question before initialization, reformulating slanted phrasings into neutral research questions.

Output is evidence-oriented rather than probability-oriented. The verdict is graded on evidence weight — Strong, Moderate, Weak, Insufficient — rather than on probability YES/NO. The visual mode is light. The Guardian safety criteria emphasize academic integrity — citation accuracy, retraction handling, and methodological transparency.

Letters and Science is described in detail in Section 13.

## 10.3 Mode Toggle and Architectural Symmetry

The mode toggle is a pill at the bottom center of the home screen. Prediction Markets is dark mode, active by default. Letters and Science is light mode, currently waitlisted — tapping it opens a waitlist modal that collects email addresses for the v2 launch.

The architecture is mode-agnostic by design. The ProductMode schema, the tier_names config, the Guardian safety criteria switching, and the dual color system are all defined to carry over to v2 without rebuild. Five specific implementation decisions in v1 — session_type field, question input abstraction, modular evidence extraction, waitlist collection, and mode-driven UI text — were made to ensure v2 ships clean.
